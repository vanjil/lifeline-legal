Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

Lifeline is a personal safety application designed to help users quickly request assistance from people they trust, such as family members, friends, or caregivers.

1. Information We Collect

Lifeline does not require users to provide an email address, phone number, or password to use the app.

To provide Lifeline's functionality, we may process:

  • An anonymous technical user identifier.
  • Technical identifiers used to establish connections between Lifeline users.
  • Names entered by users for themselves or trusted contacts.
  • Trusted-contact relationships and their priority order.
  • Temporary invitation codes used to establish connections.
  • SOS request status and timestamps.
  • Location coordinates, accuracy, and capture time during an SOS when permission is granted.
  • Battery level and charging status during an SOS.
  • Push notification tokens, device platform, and technical delivery information.
  • Local settings such as selected language, alert sound, and Safety Notice confirmation.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only as necessary to operate Lifeline, including to:

  • Send and receive SOS requests.
  • Notify trusted contacts.
  • Provide location information during an SOS when permission is granted.
  • Display relevant device status during an SOS.
  • Establish and manage trusted-contact relationships.
  • Maintain SOS history.
  • Deliver push notifications.
  • Maintain app preferences and functionality.
  • Protect the reliability and security of the service.

We do not use Lifeline data for targeted advertising.

3. Sharing of Information

Information associated with an SOS request may be shared with the trusted contacts selected by the user.

For example, a trusted contact may receive the requesting user's name, location, battery status, and SOS status when those data are available.

We also use service providers necessary to operate Lifeline, including infrastructure for database, authentication, backend functionality, and push-notification delivery.

We do not sell users' personal information.

4. Location

Lifeline requests location access for the purpose of providing a requesting user's location to trusted contacts during an SOS.

If location permission is denied or location information cannot be obtained, trusted contacts may not receive the requesting user's location.

Lifeline does not use location information for advertising.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Information necessary to operate Lifeline may be stored while the user's Lifeline account remains active.

SOS records may remain available as part of the user's help history until they are removed as part of account deletion or otherwise removed by the service.

Some information may also be stored temporarily as necessary for invitation codes, notification delivery, security, reliability, or technical operation of the service.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Users can delete their Lifeline account directly from the app: Settings → Delete Account & Data.

Deleting an account removes data associated with that Lifeline account from Lifeline's systems, including applicable trusted-contact relationships, SOS information, push-notification information, profile information, invitations, and other associated application data.

Trusted-contact relationships involving the deleted account are also removed.

Local Lifeline settings associated with the installation are also cleared.

Account deletion cannot be undone.

If a person uses Lifeline again after deleting an account, Lifeline may create a new anonymous technical identifier necessary to operate the service.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed by Lifeline.

However, no internet-based service, mobile network, push-notification system, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to operate without interruption or to be completely secure.

Because Lifeline relies on internet connectivity, device functionality, notification services, and other technology, delivery of an SOS request cannot be guaranteed.

8. Emergency Services

Lifeline is designed to facilitate communication between users and their trusted contacts.

Lifeline does not automatically contact 911, police, fire departments, ambulance services, or other emergency responders.

Lifeline should not be relied upon as a substitute for emergency services.

9. Children's Privacy

Lifeline is not specifically designed or marketed as a service for children.

If applicable law requires parental or guardian consent for a person's use of Lifeline or processing of their information, the responsible adult should ensure that the required consent has been obtained.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Lifeline evolves or as legal, technical, or operational requirements change.

When we make material changes, we may update the effective date and provide appropriate notice where required.
